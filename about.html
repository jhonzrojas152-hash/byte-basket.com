HISTORY

Byte & Basket was created as a proposed convenience store business concept. The idea is to combine the familiar services of a neighborhood convenience store with practical technology that can improve customer convenience and daily operations.

The name "Byte" represents digital technology and information, while "Basket" represents shopping and everyday essentials. The concept reflects the founder's educational background and interest in information technology.

MISSION AND VISION

Mission: To provide convenient access to everyday essentials and useful digital services through organized, customer-friendly, and technology-assisted operations.

Vision: To become a trusted neighborhood convenience store recognized for combining dependable everyday service with practical and accessible technology.
