OUR SERVICES

Byte & Basket is a proposed smart convenience store. The following are the products, customer services, and technology features planned for the business.

CONVENIENCE STORE PRODUCTS

  • Snacks, biscuits, and ready-to-eat food.
  • Bottled drinks and beverages.
  • Basic grocery items and household necessities.
  • Personal care and other everyday essentials.

DIGITAL AND TECHNOLOGY SERVICES

  • Cashless payment options through supported QR and e-wallet services.
  • Pay-per-use Peso WiFi internet access.
  • Prepaid mobile load services.
  • CCTV monitoring for store security.
  • Digital point-of-sale (POS) records for sales.
  • Digital inventory monitoring to help track product stocks.

CUSTOMER CONVENIENCE

  • Friendly customer assistance.
  • Organized product displays.
  • Clear information about available products and services.
